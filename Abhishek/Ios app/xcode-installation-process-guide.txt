================================================================================
                XCODE INSTALLATION PROCESS GUIDE FOR MACOS
================================================================================

METHOD 1: OFFICIAL MAC APP STORE INSTALLATION (RECOMMENDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Boot into your macOS desktop (Physical Mac or macOS Virtual Machine).
2. Click the "App Store" icon from the Dock or Launchpad.
3. Search for "Xcode" in the top-left search bar and press Enter.
4. Click the "Get" / "Install" button.
   - Xcode is 100% Free provided by Apple.
   - You will need to sign in with your free Apple ID if prompted.
5. Wait for Xcode to download and install (approx. 7 GB to 10 GB file size).
6. Open Xcode from your Applications folder.
7. On first launch, a prompt will state: "Xcode requires additional components".
   - Click "Install" and enter your macOS administrator password.
8. Select "iOS" from the platform selection checklist and click Install.


METHOD 2: DIRECT DOWNLOAD FROM APPLE DEVELOPER PORTAL (ALTERNATIVE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Safari browser on your macOS and navigate to:
   https://developer.apple.com/download/all/
2. Sign in with your free Apple ID account.
3. Search for "Xcode 15.4" or "Xcode 16.0".
4. Click to download the compressed `.xip` archive file (e.g., Xcode_15.4.xip).
5. Once downloaded, double-click the `.xip` file in your Downloads folder to uncompress.
6. Drag the extracted `Xcode.app` icon directly into your `Applications` folder.
7. Double-click `Xcode.app` to launch and complete the additional components install.


METHOD 3: COMMAND LINE TOOLS INSTALLATION (TERMINAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To install the essential Xcode Command Line compiler tools via Terminal:
1. Open Terminal app inside macOS.
2. Type the following command and press Enter:
   xcode-select --install
3. Click "Install" on the pop-up dialog to confirm installation.


CHECKING XCODE INSTALLATION SUCCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Terminal in macOS and run:
   xcodebuild -version
2. Expected Output:
   Xcode 15.4 (Build 15F31d) or Xcode 16.0
3. Now Xcode is completely installed and ready to build your SwiftUI iOS App!
